About Little Wonder

Some ideas stay with you long after you close the book.

Little Wonder is a free reading room for the curious. It brings together philosophy, literary history, overlooked moments from the past, the natural world, counterintuitive science, and questions we have not yet answered.

Each discovery takes about a minute. Read one, follow its sources, or sit with the question at the end. There is no finish line, streak to protect, or account to create.

Made for a slower moment

The illustrated atlas is an invitation to pause, not another feed to keep up with. Sixty original launch discoveries live here in six collections. This is a small, independently maintained project by Melvin Varghese.

Have a correction or a source worth adding? Leave a note in the public project repository. A GitHub account is needed to post there; reading this site never requires one. Please identify the discovery and include a reliable source.
