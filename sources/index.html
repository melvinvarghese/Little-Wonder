Notes from Little Wonder

A note on sources

Curiosity deserves something solid to stand on.

Every discovery includes direct links to sources and further reading. We prefer primary texts and the work of universities, scientific institutions, museums, and libraries. The articles are original summaries, not copied passages.

What we know, and what we do not

Philosophical arguments are presented as arguments, not settled facts. Historical uncertainty is labelled where it matters. The Unanswered separates evidence from proposed explanations. A short reading cannot capture every scholarly debate, so follow the sources for nuance.

Artwork

The specimen illustrations were generated specifically for Little Wonder. They are interpretive companions to the readings, not historical artifacts, reconstructions, or scientific diagrams. The tiny-window mark was drawn for this project. All artwork and the open-source Lora and Karla fonts are stored with the website; no external image or font service is contacted while you read.

Corrections

If something seems wrong, please open a correction in the project repository with the discovery title and a supporting reference. Source pages can move; their names are included so you can still find them.
