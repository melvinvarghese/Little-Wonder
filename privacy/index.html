Notes from Little Wonder

Your curiosity is private

No account. No tracking. No one looking over your shoulder.

What stays in your browser

Little Wonder stores the identifiers of your saved discoveries in localStorage under “little-wonder-collection-v1”. A last-surprise identifier may be kept in sessionStorage to avoid repeating the same random discovery. Search runs on your device. We do not send favourites, search words, or collection imports to a server.

An exported collection is a JSON file containing a format version and saved identifiers. Importing reads that file on your device and merges it with your current collection. Nothing is uploaded. Keep a backup if your favourites matter to you: browser storage is not permanent and does not sync across devices.

No analytics or advertising

This site sets no tracking cookies and loads no advertising, analytics, external fonts, or third-party images. GitHub Pages hosts the files and may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement. Following a source link takes you to another site with its own policies.

You are in control

Remove individual favourites using “Remove from my collection”. You can clear all local information through your browser’s site-data settings. Export first if you want to keep it. If storage is unavailable or corrupted, the site explains what happened rather than silently overwriting it.
